▸ ℹ️ How Auto-fill, Shuffle and Fit portions work

Auto-fill spreads your own meals across the week (keeping to the repeat limit) and adds a snack or two, then tries swapping in other meals wherever that brings a day closer to your targets. For each day it works out the portion sizes (🔗 ratio ingredients scale together) and ↕ range amounts that land on your calories first, then protein, carbs and fat. 📌 fixed ones never change. Picked meals yourself? Hit ⚖️ Fit portions. Don't fancy the whole layout? 🎲 Shuffle week deals the meals out differently each time. Don't fancy one meal? Its own 🎲 swaps it for a different one that still fits the day. The small box is the portion size; the Ready tab turns it all into grams.

5. Already at Home
